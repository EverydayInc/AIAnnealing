# Annealing + Pareto Toolkit Author Rights Agreement

This Author Rights Agreement (the **Agreement**) is between **Everyday Inc** (the **Owner**) and **Todd Beckett** (the **Author**). The Agreement becomes effective on the date of the last signature below (the **Effective Date**).

## 1. Definitions

1. **Toolkit** means the Annealing + Pareto Toolkit, including its methods, documentation, prompts, agent and skill definitions, schemas, scripts, tests, fixtures, archives, and later versions that the Owner owns.
2. **Author Contribution** means a modification or addition that the Author creates and that the Owner owns and incorporates into the Toolkit.
3. **Author Work** means the original Toolkit, each Author Contribution, and any modification, derivative work, fork, or successor work that the Author creates from them.
4. **Owner Rights** means all copyrights, patent rights, trade-secret rights, and other intellectual-property rights in the Toolkit that the Owner owns or has authority to license.

## 2. Authorship and ownership

1. The Owner recognizes the Author as the original author of the Toolkit.
2. The Owner retains its ownership of the Toolkit and the Owner Rights.
3. This Agreement grants rights to the Author. It does not transfer the Owner's ownership of the original Toolkit.
4. The Author owns an Author Work to the extent that the Owner does not own it under a separate written agreement or applicable law.

## 3. Grant to the Author

1. The Owner grants the Author a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, no-charge, royalty-free, fully paid, irrevocable license under the Owner Rights.
2. The Author may use, copy, reproduce, modify, maintain, fork, and continue development of the Toolkit.
3. The Author may create, use, host, embed, make available, publish, publicly display, publicly perform, distribute, sell, import, and otherwise exploit an Author Work for any lawful purpose.
4. The Author may grant sublicenses and may apply different license terms to an Author Work.
5. The Author may use the rights for commercial or noncommercial purposes and in source or object form.
6. The Author may exercise the rights independently of the Owner. The Author has no duty to obtain further approval, contribute changes to the Owner, disclose source, use the Owner's infrastructure, or preserve the Toolkit's name or branding in an Author Work.

## 4. Scope and duration

1. The grant applies to the Toolkit that exists on the Effective Date.
2. The grant automatically applies to each later Author Contribution when the Owner incorporates it into the Toolkit.
3. The grant survives any later change to the Toolkit's public license.
4. The grant survives the Owner's decision to stop developing or distributing the Toolkit.
5. The grant survives the end of any employment, contractor, officer, shareholder, or other relationship between the Owner and the Author.
6. The Owner must not revoke, terminate, narrow, or condition the grant because of such a change or termination.

## 5. Author attribution

1. The Author may identify himself as the original author of the Toolkit.
2. The Owner may continue to identify the Author accurately in the Toolkit and related records.
3. Neither party may state that the other party endorses a later Author Work without written consent.

## 6. Third-party rights and law

1. The Owner grants only the rights that the Owner owns or has authority to license.
2. This Agreement does not grant rights in third-party material.
3. The Author must comply with applicable law when the Author exercises the grant.
